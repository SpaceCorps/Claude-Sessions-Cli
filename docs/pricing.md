# Pricing & Licensing

## 100% Free & Open Source

The **Claude Sessions CLI** (`claude-sessions`) is entirely free software distributed under the permissive [MIT License](https://github.com/SpaceCorps/Claude-Sessions-Cli/blob/main/LICENSE).

- **No Subscription Tiers:** All features, commands, and options are completely unlocked for all users.
- **No Cloud Fees:** The tool runs locally on your workstation with no hosted backend or SaaS component.
- **Commercial & Enterprise Use:** You are free to use, distribute, integrate, and modify this tool within commercial organizations and internal developer tooling.

SpaceCorps develops high-performance systems software, autonomous agent tooling, and developer infrastructure in pure Rust. All SpaceCorps CLI tools are freely available to the global engineering community.
